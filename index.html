The Charter Office

Type a name and this office engraves you a commemorative bank charter for The Standard Reserve, a protocol that has not opened yet.

built by @flxrnc

What this is

The Standard Reserve is a protocol where holding a charter makes you a banker. It has not launched. This office prints a commemorative version of that charter with your name on it, and an optional examination about how the protocol works decides its stamp.

Where things stand

The Standard Reserve has not launched. There is no live token and no live NFT, so anything selling $STANDARD today is fake.

What this office is

Unofficial. It connects no wallet, asks for nothing, and issues nothing the protocol recognises.
